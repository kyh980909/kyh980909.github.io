A little about me

Curious about what makes AI reliable outside the lab.

My research connects computer vision, uncertainty, and efficient computing. I enjoy turning careful experiments into models that are easier to trust—and practical enough to leave the benchmark behind. I’m also exploring how reliable learning can transfer safely across different embodied systems.

What I’m exploring

Current research interests

My current work asks when to trust a prediction, how to recognize the unfamiliar, how to run efficiently, and how reliable learning can move into the physical world.

◌

Trustworthy & Uncertainty-Aware AI

Building models with calibrated confidence, interpretable uncertainty, and greater robustness under distribution shift and adversarial inputs.

Calibration · uncertainty · robustness

⌁

Open-World Visual Recognition

Helping visual models recognize known and unseen objects through open-vocabulary learning, OOD detection, and anomaly detection.

Open vocabulary · OOD · anomaly detection

↯

Efficient Edge Vision

Optimizing real-time visual intelligence for resource-constrained accelerators and practical deployment environments.

Real-time vision · accelerators · deployment

◎

Physical AI & Cross-Embodiment Learning

An emerging research direction exploring safe knowledge transfer across heterogeneous robots and embodied systems.

Emerging direction · transfer · embodied systems
